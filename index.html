Your day, without the noise.

Almanac is a to-do app for Android. Notes and habits sit beside your tasks, and you pick which of the three you actually want to use.

Status

Almanac is in closed testing on Google Play. This site is where its privacy policy and account deletion route live.

What it does

To do

Tasks with dates

Reminders, repeats, priority, subtasks, notes and attachments, in color-coded lists.

Notes

Notes and a vault

Plain notes in folders, plus a vault locked by a PIN or your fingerprint.

Habits

Habits and streaks

Four kinds of schedule, checked off day by day, with streaks that survive rest days.

Your account

You sign in with Google. Your tasks, lists, notes and habits are saved to that account, so they come back when you reinstall or use another phone.

Vault notes and the photos and files you attach stay on your phone and are never uploaded. Almanac has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and your content is never sold or shared.
