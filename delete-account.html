Delete your Almanac account

Two ways to do it. No reason needed, and nobody will try to talk you out of it.

From inside the app

  1. Open Almanac and go to the Today tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon in the top right corner.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It happens straight away. You are signed out and returned to the sign-in screen.

If you no longer have the app

Email tahalakdawala03@gmail.com from the Google address your account uses, and say you want your Almanac account deleted. We reply within 7 days, and the deletion itself is done within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens to it
Your account: name, email address, profile picture and Google account IDPermanently deleted
Your tasks, lists and subtasksPermanently deleted
Your notes and folders held in the accountPermanently deleted
Your habits and their check-insPermanently deleted
The markers left behind by items you deleted earlierPermanently deleted

Backups of the database are overwritten on their own schedule, so a copy can survive there for a short while after the account itself is gone.

Delete your content but keep your account

If you only want your tasks, lists, notes and habits erased, and not the account itself: open Almanac, go to Settings › Delete all data and confirm. It clears the app on this phone, vault notes and attachments included, and erases the same content from your account straight away. Your account stays, empty, so you can keep using the app.

If you no longer have the app, email tahalakdawala03@gmail.com from the Google address your account uses and say you want your content erased but your account kept.

What stays on your phone

Deleting the account does not wipe the phone you are holding. Your vault notes and your attachments were never in the account to begin with, and the rest of the app's data stays on the phone until you remove it.

To clear the phone too, use Settings › Delete all data, or uninstall Almanac.
